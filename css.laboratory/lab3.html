Readable Typography and Color

Good typography and color choices make a page easier to scan and read without eye strain. Generous line spacing gives lines room to breathe so the eye tracks smoothly from one line to the next.

Why Contrast and Spacing Matter

High contrast between text and background keeps words crisp and legible across different screens and lighting conditions. Pairing a calm, light background with dark text avoids harsh glare while keeping content clear.

Consistent hierarchy helps readers spot main topics and subtopics right away. When headings, body paragraphs, lists, and links look distinct, visitors can find what they need quickly.

  • Keep body text around 16px for comfortable reading.
  • Use a line height near 1.5 to 1.6 for paragraphs and lists.
  • Maintain strong contrast between text and background colors.
  • Style links clearly so interactive elements are easy to recognize.
